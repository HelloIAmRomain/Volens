## Full description — 4 000 characters maximum

```
You never control an agent. You write the law that governs them all.

Volens is a puzzle game. In each level you assemble a stack of a few behaviour rules — move toward the light, flee danger, stay grouped, spread out, hug the wall — and above all, you ORDER them. Then you run the simulation and watch what happens.

ORDER IS PRIORITY

For every agent, at every moment, the first applicable rule wins. That sentence contains the whole game.

The same rules in two different orders produce two swarms with nothing in common. "Flee danger" above "move toward the light" gives a careful swarm that goes around. The reverse gives a swarm that charges and leaves agents behind. You changed nothing but the order.

WHAT YOU ACTUALLY DO

You give no orders during the action: once the simulation runs, there is nothing to tap. You place no towers, you aim at nothing, you need no reflexes.

You design a system, you let it go, and you find out what it produces — including when it produces the opposite of what you intended. That is usually the moment the level explains itself.

A SIMULATION THAT NEVER CHEATS

Nothing is random. No dice, no hidden variation, no difficulty quietly adjusting behind your back. The same level and the same stack give exactly the same result, every time, on any device.

When a swarm fails, luck had nothing to do with it. It was your stack, and your stack can be read.

FAILING COSTS NOTHING

No lives, no energy, no attempt counter. You can pause, step forward one tick at a time, REWIND to the start of the run, change your stack and go again. As often as you like.

A failure is information, not a punishment.

BUILT FOR TEN MINUTES ON A BUS

One hand, portrait. The game resumes exactly where you left it, even days later. No run is ever lost because you reached your stop.

WHAT IS NOT IN IT

No ads. No data collection. No account to create. No internet connection — the game does not even ask the system for permission to open one. No timers, no notifications, no daily rewards, nothing engineered to pull you back.

Volens does not know who you are, and has no way of finding out.

THE FIRST CHAPTER IS FREE

All of it, with no purchase prompt while you play it. If you like the game, the rest unlocks once, for good.
```

*(2 252 characters of 4 000 — dense rather than long.)*
