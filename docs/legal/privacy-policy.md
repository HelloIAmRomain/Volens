# Privacy Policy — Volens

**Last updated: 6 September 2026**

Volens is a puzzle game for Android, developed by **Draille**.

## In one sentence

**Volens collects no data about you, transmits none, and connects to no
server.**

This is not a statement of intent: it is a property of the application, and it
can be verified. This document explains what that means in practice.

## What data is collected?

**None.**

Volens asks for no name, no email address, no account, no sign-up. It does not
read your contacts, your location, your files, your address book or your
advertising identifier. It does not know who you are, and has no way of finding
out.

## What is stored on your device?

The game saves three things, **on your device only**, in the private storage
area Android reserves for the application:

| File | Contents |
|---|---|
| `progress.json` | levels solved and challenges earned |
| `settings.json` | your settings (theme, haptics, language) |
| `session.json` | the game in progress, so you can resume where you left off |

These files never leave your device. No other application can read them.
**Uninstalling Volens deletes them permanently**, and that is the only action
needed to erase every trace of the game.

## Network access

**Volens never accesses the internet.** The application does not request
Android's network permission, which means the operating system itself prevents
it from opening any connection — regardless of what the code might attempt. The
game runs entirely offline, including on first launch.

## Permissions requested

One: **`VIBRATE`**, for haptic feedback during interactions.

This is a *normal*-level permission: Android grants it without a dialog and it
gives access to no data whatsoever. It is used only to vibrate the device, and
haptic feedback can be turned off in the game's settings.

Volens requests **no other permission**. None for network, shared storage,
camera, microphone, location, contacts or notifications.

## Third-party services, advertising, analytics

**None.**

Volens embeds no third-party SDK, no advertising network, no analytics or
telemetry tool, no crash-reporting service. Nothing in the application reports
your usage to anyone, not even to its author.

## Purchases

If a future version offers a purchase to unlock content, the transaction is
handled **entirely by Google Play**. The developer receives neither your name,
nor your address, nor any payment data — only Google's confirmation that a
purchase took place.

Google's handling of your data during a purchase is governed by
[Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy), over which we
have no control.

## Children

Volens collects no data, from anyone, of any age. The game contains no chat, no
user-generated content, and no links to the outside world.

## Your rights

Since no personal data is collected or retained, there is nothing for us to
provide, correct, export or delete — data protection law has no purchase on
data that does not exist. The local files described above are under your sole
control and disappear with the application.

## Changes to this policy

Should the application change in a way that makes this document inaccurate, it
will be updated **before** the affected version is published, and the date at
the top of this page changed accordingly.
